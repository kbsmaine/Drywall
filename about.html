About us

Maine-built experience.

J.P. Burnham Drywall is a Buxton-based drywall company focused on dependable work, clean finishes, and straightforward service.

More than two decades

Hands-on experience in every room.

For more than 20 years, J.P. Burnham Drywall has worked in Maine homes and construction projects where details matter. The company handles everything from straightforward board installation to demanding vaulted ceilings, tight stairwells, detailed transitions, and large open interiors.

The approach is practical: show up prepared, coordinate with the project, keep the work moving, and deliver a surface that gives painters and homeowners a strong final result.
